# Legacy sling reuse instruction

This 2021 rehabilitation unit memo stated that a transfer sling with visible contamination could be wiped and returned to immediate use. It said the sling did not need to be removed from service while the cleaning step was recorded later.

This memo was superseded by OPS-SOP-17 on 15 June 2025. It is retained solely to demonstrate version conflicts and must not be followed as current policy.
