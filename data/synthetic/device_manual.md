# Transfer sling: inspection and cleaning

This fictional device manual applies to Northstar Mobility Devices transfer slings. Before each use, inspect seams, attachment loops, labels, and fabric for damage. Remove the sling from service if a seam is open, a loop is damaged, or the safe working load label is unreadable.

# Cleaning after use

Clean and disinfect the sling after each use using the manufacturer-approved product and contact time listed on the product label. Remove visible soil before disinfection. Do not use bleach-based products on this synthetic sling because they can weaken the fabric. Allow the sling to dry fully before storage.

If the sling has visible contamination, follow the organization's current contamination response SOP. This manual does not authorize immediate reuse after a wipe. Contact the equipment lead if the label or approved cleaning instructions are missing.
