# Immediate response to visible contamination

Effective 15 June 2025, remove a transfer sling with visible contamination from service immediately. Do not reuse it until cleaning and disinfection have been completed according to the device manual and the action has been documented. Attach an out-of-service notice and contact the equipment lead if the sling cannot be cleaned using an approved method.

# Routine turnover

Between uses, complete the cleaning steps in the current device manual and document the check in the approved equipment record. A visual wipe alone is not evidence that required cleaning and disinfection are complete. When the device manual is unavailable, keep the equipment out of service and contact the equipment lead.

# Escalation and records

Notify Infection Prevention when the contamination source is unknown, when an exposure may have occurred, or when the approved cleaning process is unclear. Record the equipment identifier, the action taken, and the date in the operational record. Do not enter patient names or medical record numbers in general equipment notes.
