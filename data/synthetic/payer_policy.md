# Mobility equipment coverage criteria

This fictional payer policy applies to claims submitted to Example Mutual. Coverage review requires a current order, a documented functional assessment, and evidence that the requested equipment matches the documented need. A prior authorization decision is required for Transfer Sling Model B.

This policy does not establish clinical suitability and does not replace the organization's equipment approval or infection prevention process. For claim submission questions, contact the billing policy team. The policy is version 2025.2, effective 1 September 2025.
