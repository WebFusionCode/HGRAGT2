# Asking for mobility assistance

This fictional patient guide explains how to ask staff for help with a transfer. Tell the care team if your comfort, strength, or ability to move has changed since the last assessment. Ask staff to explain the planned movement before it begins.

If equipment looks damaged or you are unsure whether it is ready, pause and tell a staff member. Staff will check the equipment and your care plan. Do not try to use lifting equipment on your own.

This guide does not assess an individual's condition or replace advice from their care team.
